# Commodity Trading Journal 📈

A systematic record of active paper trading and market analysis focused on 
energy commodities — primarily Brent crude oil and WTI. Each entry documents 
trade rationale, market drivers, risk management decisions and lessons learned.

## Purpose

Building genuine commodity trading knowledge and market intuition through 
disciplined paper trading on Trading212, combined with Python-based price 
analysis. Designed to demonstrate real commercial thinking and market awareness 
to recruiters in energy trading and asset management.

## Repository Contents

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `trading_journal.md` | Weekly trade entries with rationale and outcomes |
| `app.py` | Python price analysis with annotated trade entry charts |
| `brent_trade_entry.png` | Brent crude price chart with trade annotations |
| `Trade screenshots` | Trading212 position screenshots with timestamps |
